Criterion 1 ✓ — Proprietary data
The episodic vault: institutional memory that compounds
The vault is an episodic record system, not a document store. Every compliance decision is stored as a structured event: what was decided, why, when, where it applies, who approved it, what state the evidence was in. Jurisdiction is a record property — not a query filter. Approved claim wording is preserved verbatim. Temporal integrity means expired evidence triggers automatic re-evaluation.

Clients who leave retain their audit trail data but lose the ongoing learning engine. That asymmetry is the switching cost — not engineering time, but irreplaceable institutional history. This is the Harvey AI model in legal services applied to marketing compliance.
Criterion 2 ✓ — Orchestration
Three-tier architecture with compounding flywheel
Tier One: deterministic checks (substantiation gaps, regulatory violations, jurisdictional scope) — automated, high confidence. Tier Two: borderline and novel cases — AI assessment with mandatory human sign-off. Tier Three: genuinely novel territory — human-led decisions recorded as new precedent.

The flywheel: every Tier Two decision writes back to the vault as a structured episode. Over time, Tier Two decisions migrate to Tier One. The system handles more automatically. Human reviewers focus on judgement calls. The flywheel runs in both directions — overrides trigger reclassification, preventing confident errors. This integration depth takes years to replicate, not better engineering.
Criterion 3 ✓ — Regulatory permission
Truth Marketing® + certifications: irreplicable by capital
SOC2 + ISO27001 arriving within three months. Truth Marketing® as the certification standard for AI-governed marketing — recognised by CLOs, mandatable in procurement agreements, citable in regulatory responses.

A certification standard is not a technology. OpenAI cannot ship a feature that substitutes for it. With every Shein fine, every Armani ruling, every ASA enforcement action — the value of the institution that holds this track record compounds. A competitor can only start later.
What we deliberately deprioritise
Brief generation, regulatory summarisation, and tone-of-voice guidance as standalone features: these are commoditising at speed. We lead with vault depth, governance workflow, and Truth Marketing® certification — all three of which foundation models cannot absorb. We do not compete on output quality. We compete on institutional memory, legal defensibility, and audit trail.

Section 10 — Risks & Mitigations
Five risks that could
specifically kill this business.
Assessed on likelihood × impact. Each risk names the early warning sign and the concrete mitigation. We do not manage risk by ignoring it — we manage it by seeing it early enough to act.
RiskRatingEarly warning signMitigation
Foundation model commoditisation
OpenAI ships GPT-6 with native compliance checking. Brief generation and regulatory summarisation value erodes overnight.
 HIGH × HIGH GPT announcements referencing "brand governance." Prospects in RFPs comparing Flourish to "just GPT-5 with our guidelines." Already mitigated structurally. We deprioritise brief generation. We lead with vault depth, episodic memory, multi-user sign-off, and Truth Marketing® — all three of which foundation models cannot absorb. Every client conversation leads with "what cannot be replicated," not "what we generate."
Runway exhaustion before seed round
£50k/month burn with £240k ARR. Two sales from breakeven. A three-month sales slip creates a critical window.
 HIGH × HIGH Runway below 4 months before June contract expected. WFA produces no pipeline. First enterprise contract delayed past June. Mitigation 1: Convert one warm prospect (Barilla, Asahi, or Betway) before WFA — reduces dependence on event pipeline. Mitigation 2: Founder salary reduction agreement pre-agreed as a reversible lever. Mitigation 3: Bridge loan option available via Jo + Charlotte. Mitigation 4: R&D tax credit accelerates cash.
Vault quality / cold start problem
New deployments have sparse vaults. If clients do not invest in vault quality, the three-tier architecture does not compound and the moat does not deepen.
 MED × HIGH Tier Two override rate >40%. Client NPS falls in months 3–6. Clients treating Flourish as a document store. Vault Steward role: Every deployment designates a Steward with authority to manage the evidence base. Onboarding investment: First six months treated as a vault-building exercise. Pricing incentive: Success fees tied to vault episode count and Tier One migration rate.
Regulatory urgency evaporates
EU AI Act enforcement softer than expected. Urgency argument that drives sales cycles weakens.
 LOW × HIGH Enforcement actions slow post-August 2026. Board-level compliance urgency reduces. Diversified value proposition: Regulatory urgency is an accelerant, not the sole reason to buy. Speed (94% faster sign-off), vault depth as institutional memory, and the confidence infrastructure case all stand independently. UK + EU + North America means no single enforcement calendar determines our pipeline.
Agency channel creates Frankenstein product
Agency partners with 15–30 brand clients each, all with different requirements, pull the product in conflicting directions before it is stable.
 MED × MED Agency feedback creates conflicting wish-list requests. Product roadmap velocity drops. Core clients complain about feature dilution. Hard gate: Agency channel does not open before September 2026. Training first: Agencies trained on "this condition, this problem, this environment" — they know what Flourish is not for. Mike Lander qualifies every partner: No agency signed without his assessment of product fit. Never white-label — we retain all three control points.

EU AI Act · Article 50
Phase 1

Article 50 Readiness Audit

A brand submits an existing campaign asset or batch of assets to Flourish. The vault checks every claim in the asset against three things: whether the claim has a valid, substantiated equivalent in the vault; whether the asset discloses AI involvement as Article 50 requires for the target market; and whether the disclosure format is correct for the asset's regional operating context. The output is an Article 50 Readiness Report — claim by claim, with a clear status for each: compliant, at risk, or non-compliant. Recommended fixes point directly to the vault's approved claim library. The report is both a gap analysis and a due diligence record.

Submit your assets. Know your Article 50 exposure. Fix it before August.

EU Product Liability Directive
Phase 1

Claims Defence Bundle

Under the revised PLD, if a consumer is harmed by a product claim, the brand must produce the evidence trail or courts can presume the product was defective. One click from any content asset or vault claim generates a court-ready evidence bundle: the claim text, every substantiation link, the underlying reference documents, the approval record with timestamps, the AI involvement flag, and the regional operating context. Not a report for internal use. A legally formatted document designed for disclosure to a regulator or court. The vault already holds every component. This feature is packaging, not new infrastructure.

Your litigation defence is already assembled. One click to produce it.

EU AI Act · GPAI · Article 50
Phase 1

Vault-Grounded Prompt Generator

Most marketers prompt ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini in the wild — outside any governed system, with no guardrails on which claims they can make. This feature generates a vault-grounded AI prompt for any project, pre-loaded exclusively with the brand's approved claims for that operating context, with the Article 50 disclosure instruction embedded, and with explicit guardrails against prohibited AI outputs. The marketer copies the prompt, opens their AI tool of choice, and pastes it in. The vault does not control the AI tool. It controls what the human brings to it. Governance at the point of prompting, not after the fact.

Compliance at the point of prompting. Meets marketers where they actually work.

EU AI Act · IP · Commercial licensing
Phase 2

AI Tool Risk Register

Brands are using ChatGPT, Claude, Midjourney, Adobe Firefly, and a dozen other AI tools to produce campaign assets with no visibility into the legal implications of each one. Every tool has a different position on commercial licensing, copyright ownership, training data transparency, and EU AI Act compliance status. Flourish maintains a live AI Tool Risk Register as a vault layer, updated continuously as tool ToS changes and regulatory guidance evolves. When a team submits an asset and declares which tools were used, the vault cross-references the register and returns a clear picture: ownership status, disclosure obligations, and compliance flags by market.

We used ChatGPT and Midjourney for this campaign. Do we own it? Are we compliant? Flourish answers in 30 seconds.

ASA · FTC · EU national authorities
Phase 3

Regulatory Claims Precedent Library

When the vault validates a claim, it does not just check internal substantiation against the brand's own references. It surfaces external regulatory precedent: enforcement decisions, upheld complaints, and challenged claims from the ASA, FTC, ARPP, and other national authorities where the same or comparable claims were brought before a regulator. A claim does not exist in isolation — it exists in a regulatory environment with a history. Knowing that a structurally similar claim was upheld in 2024 or challenged in Germany in 2025, and exactly what evidence gap caused the challenge, is intelligence no internal vault can produce on its own.

"Clinically proven to reduce fine lines by 40%" — Flourish returns three analogous ASA decisions and the specific evidence gap that caused each challenge.

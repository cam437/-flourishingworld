The Regulatory Reality

The most regulated marketing
environment on earth.

A Betway marketer doesn't face one rulebook. They face dozens — each contradicting the last, each shifting faster than any approval process can handle.

United Kingdom

Gambling Act White Paper

  • Celebrity & influencer bans in gambling ads
  • Whistle-to-whistle advertising restrictions
  • Gambling Commission prior approval for certain formats
  • CAP/BCAP gambling-specific ad standards
European Markets

Fragmented National Regimes

  • Germany: extreme advertising restrictions, blackout hours
  • Netherlands: total ban on untargeted ads since 2023
  • Belgium: near-total gambling ad ban
  • Italy: blanket gambling advertising ban
Global

Safer Gambling Obligations

  • Market-specific responsible gambling messaging
  • Geolocation and age-gating for digital
  • FTC truth-in-advertising rules (US states)
New · 2026

AI Content Laws

  • EU AI Act: mandatory disclosure, provenance, human oversight
  • ASA guidance on AI-generated gambling content
  • DSA/GDPR: automated targeting restrictions

Every campaign requires sign-off against multiple, overlapping, contradictory regulatory frameworks — before a single word reaches a consumer. The legal review process was never designed for this.

The 2026 AI Content Laws

The AI Act changed how you
market with AI.

AI-generated marketing carries its own legal obligations — disclosure, provenance, human oversight, audit trails. Most platforms ignore this. Flourish is built around it.

EU AI Act · In Force

Transparency & Disclosure

AI content must be labelled. Gambling faces enhanced requirements. Providers must maintain documentation and demonstrate human oversight.

UK · ASA 2025

Sector-Specific AI Standards

AI-generated gambling ads must meet truth-in-advertising standards with additional vulnerability protections. Proof of human review expected.

Global · DSA + FTC

Targeting & Personalisation Limits

Algorithmic transparency for automated personalisation. AI-generated claims must meet existing truth standards. Every Betway market has a version of this.

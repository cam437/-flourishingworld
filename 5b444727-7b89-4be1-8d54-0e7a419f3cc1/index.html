The gaps that need to be filled

The Magnum Ice Cream Company has invested heavily in marketing compliance. That delivers real value today. But significant gaps remain — and they are costing efficiency across the wider marketing operation.

Upstream
No upstream layer.
Compliance review begins only once creative is built. Issues surface late. Revisions stack up. Brave campaigns are diluted to safe — or killed before they launch.
Workflow
Excel and workshops.
Repetitive admin consumes team capacity. Institutional knowledge does not compound. Every campaign starts the cycle from zero.
Scale
Legal coverage is EU-focused.
Other markets run on different systems, or none. The company cannot move at one speed globally. The existing investment does not scale beyond Europe.
Intelligence
CTI and Marketing run in parallel.
Briefs are not grounded in audience intelligence at the point of creation. Related work is produced twice, without a shared verified source.
Governance
Limited audit trail. No global localisation layer.
Decisions are harder to defend if challenged. Translation and adaptation work doubles back through manual review in each market.
